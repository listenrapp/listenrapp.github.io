Support

Questions, problems or a page Listenr reads badly? Email listenr.support@icloud.com. Including the link you tried helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I add something to listen to?

Copy a link or some text, open Listenr and tap Paste. In Safari you can also tap Share › Listenr, check the preview, then choose Add next or Add to end of queue. Shared ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini conversation links work too.

A link says “Couldn't connect”.

Links need an internet connection to import. Check your connection and tap Try again. Copied text can be added offline, and anything already in your queue plays offline.

Listenr read menus or captions, or skipped part of a page.

Some sites hide their text or mix it with other content. Copy the part you want and paste it instead, and please email us the link so we can improve how that site is read.

Does it work with the screen locked?

Yes. Playback continues when you lock your iPhone, and the lock screen, Control Center, AirPods and car controls all work.

Which languages does Listenr read?

English. If text you add looks like another language, Listenr warns you that pronunciation may be incorrect, and you can still add it.

How does Listenr Pro work?

Listening is free for 15 minutes a day at up to 1.5×. Listenr Pro is a monthly subscription that includes unlimited listening, all playback speeds and highlighted text to read along. New subscribers can try it free. It renews automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel or restore Listenr Pro?

To manage or cancel an active subscription, open Listenr and tap Settings › Listenr Pro. You can also manage subscriptions in your iPhone's Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. If you already subscribe but Listenr shows Free, tap Settings › Listenr Pro › Restore Purchases.

Is my reading private?

Your reading is stored on your iPhone and read aloud there. Opening a web link contacts that website. See the Privacy Policy.
