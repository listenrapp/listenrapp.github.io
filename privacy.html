Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-01

Listenr collects no data about you. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Listenr — the articles, links, pasted text and chats in your queue and History, your settings, and the speech Listenr generates — is stored only on your iPhone. The voice runs on your iPhone, so the text is never sent anywhere to be read aloud. Deleting Listenr, or Settings › Delete all data, removes it.

Web pages you choose

When you give Listenr a link, it loads that page directly from the website, the same way Safari would, to get the text. The website sees an ordinary visit from your iPhone. Listenr doesn't send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Listenr Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. Listenr checks whether you have Listenr Pro on your iPhone, using the purchase record Apple provides.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Listenr collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
